## Projects

- **Northern Endurance Partnership launches offshore construction at the Port of Hartlepool** — The UK's first CO2 transport-and-storage network (BP, Equinor, TotalEnergies, delivered with Saipem) began its offshore construction campaign, staging ~12,000 concrete-coated linepipe joints (~170,000 t) for a 149 km, 28-inch offshore pipeline; the network will store up to 23 Mtpa of CO2, ~20% of UK emissions. (Energy Voice, 24 Jul 2026) [link](https://www.energyvoice.com/renewables-energy-transition/ccs/601241/northern-endurance-partnership-offshore-construction/)

(Offshore Engineer, 24 Jul 2026) [link](https://www.oedigital.com/news/541499-bp-moves-indonesia-ccus-project-into-offshore-installation-phase)
- **Uniper names Worley preferred EPC contractor for the Connah's Quay low-carbon (CCGT+CCS) power project** — Uniper selected Worley to build its proposed Deeside, Wales combined-cycle gas plant (~1.1 GW, up to 1.38 GW; ~550 MW phase 1 potentially operational by 2030) with >=95% carbon capture feeding the HyNet CO2 network, following a FEED competition; a DCO decision is pending. (Carbon Herald, 23 Jul 2026) [link](https://carbonherald.com/uniper-selects-worley-for-2030-carbon-capture-power-station/)
- **Evero moves to retrofit its Ince Bio Power plant with carbon capture, joining HyNet (UK BECCS)** — Evero Energy is progressing a BECCS retrofit at Protos, Cheshire, removing ~217,000 t CO2/yr while processing ~170,000 t of waste wood, using Mitsubishi Heavy Industries capture technology and connecting to HyNet via the planned ~980 kt/yr Protos Spur pipeline; the planning application is before Cheshire West and Chester Council. (gasworld, 24 Jul 2026) [link](https://www.gasworld.com/story/evero-bioenergy-plant-moves-to-join-hynet-carbon-capture-network/2255213.article/)
- **Petrogas picks Worley for pre-FEED of the Abeona CO2 storage project, Dutch North Sea** — Petrogas E&P Netherlands appointed Worley for the pre-FEED of Abeona, a CO2 transport-and-storage scheme for hard-to-abate emitters in the North Sea Canal Area that would reuse an existing Port of Amsterdam pipeline to inject CO2 into depleted offshore reservoirs. (Offshore Energy, 23 Jul 2026) [link](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/worley-takes-pre-feed-role-on-roman-goddess-named-dutch-north-sea-ccs-project/)
