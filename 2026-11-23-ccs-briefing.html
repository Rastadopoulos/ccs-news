COP31 collapses in Antalya without an adopted cover decision — draft CCUS and carbon-removal targets fall with it

UN News; CBS News · 22 Nov 2026 · [Global]

COP31 in Antalya closed on Sunday 22 November about two days past schedule with no consensus and no adopted cover decision — the first UN climate summit to end without an overarching agreement since 2009. Carbon capture, fossil-fuel phase-out language and climate finance were among the clashes that derailed the text. Consequently the draft cover decision's first-ever quantified carbon-management goals (the proposed ~500 Mt CO2/yr CCUS target by 2035 and the 1.3–2.5 Gt/yr long-term CDR ambition tracked through the week) were not adopted, leaving CCUS scale-up without a new multilateral signal. A >90-country clean-electrification pledge was noted as partial progress; COP32 heads to Addis Ababa in 2027. This supersedes the draft-text developments briefed earlier in the week.

Alberta extends Carbon Capture Incentive Program to 2035 and allows grant payments during construction

Western Standard; CTV News · 21 Nov 2026 · [North America]

The Alberta government has tweaked its Carbon Capture Incentive Program (ACCIP): the 12% capital grant on eligible new CCUS infrastructure is extended through 2035, and companies will be able to collect part of the grant during construction — previously grants were paid only after the first year of operation. The change aligns the provincial support more closely with the federal CCUS investment tax credit and is aimed at de-risking large oil-sands and industrial CCS builds (e.g. the Pathways network). A relevant read for CO2CRC on how sub-national fiscal design accelerates FIDs.
