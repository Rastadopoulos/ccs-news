COP31's CCUS and carbon-removal targets head into final-stretch negotiations amid EU resistance

Climate Home News · 18 Nov 2026 · [Global]

As COP31 in Antalya moves toward its scheduled Friday close, the carbon-management language in the Türkiye Presidency's draft cover decision remains contested. The proposed first-ever COP goal of at least ~500 Mt CO₂/yr of CCUS capacity by 2035 (with a longer-term 1.3–2.5 Gt/yr ambition) and the broad carbon-dioxide-removal text are both resisted by the EU and a bloc of states, while ocean and climate advocates warn the deal must not open the door to marine geoengineering and open-ended CDR. The Presidency maintains the draft reflects divergent positions, not consensus; whether the CCUS/CDR provisions survive the final plenary is the key question for the global CCS policy frame. Builds on the first-draft targets briefed 18 Nov — this is the negotiation development, not the original draft.
