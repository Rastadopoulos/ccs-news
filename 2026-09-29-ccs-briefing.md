## Projects

- **CNPC passes 13 Mt of cumulative CO2 injected as it pushes CCUS commercialisation** — China National Petroleum Corp says it has now injected more than 13 million tonnes of CO2 to date — over 60% of the Chinese petroleum sector's total — with first-half 2026 injection of 1.37 Mt (up 14.2% year-on-year), mostly via CO2-EOR; it plans integrated industrial CCUS clusters in the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026–30), with the Junggar Basin area estimated to hold up to 2 Gt of storage. (Carbon Herald, 28 Sep 2026) [link](https://carbonherald.com/cnpc-injects-over-13m-tons-of-co2-in-ccus-commercialization-push/)
- **US BLM offers Tallgrass a right-of-way grant for Southeast Wyoming CO2 storage** — The US Bureau of Land Management has offered Tallgrass Energy a right-of-way grant to use 480 acres of federally managed underground pore space in Laramie County for its Southeast Wyoming CO2 Sequestration Project, advancing commercial use of federal pore space; final Class VI injection-well permits from the Wyoming Department of Environmental Quality are still required before injection can begin. (Carbon Herald, 28 Sep 2026) [link](https://carbonherald.com/wyoming-carbon-storage-hub-gains-federal-approval/)

## Policy

- **ICVCM approves Verra's CCS methodology under the Core Carbon Principles** — The Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market approved Verra's carbon-capture-and-storage methodology as meeting its Core Carbon Principles, giving CCS-based credits access to the high-integrity CCP label — a quality signal for CCS crediting in the voluntary market. (Carbon Pulse, 28 Sep 2026) [paywall] [link](https://carbon-pulse.com/554897/)

## Technology

- **Revcoo brings all-electric cryogenic capture to a Belgian zinc plant in its first metals-sector deal** — French carbon-capture firm Revcoo will deploy its fully electric cryogenic "CarbonCloud" system at recycled-zinc producer Reazn's alloy plant in Kruisem, Belgium, targeting 5,000–8,000 tpa of 99.99%-purity CO2 without solvents or water; the partners begin with a joint technical and economic feasibility study and FEED, with full operation slated for late 2028 — Revcoo's first move beyond lime and cement into metals. (gasworld, 28 Sep 2026) [link](https://www.gasworld.com/story/revcoo-to-capture-up-to-8000-tonnes-of-co2-at-belgian-zinc-plant/2260346.article/)
