## Projects

- **Europe's largest industrial carbon-capture facility inaugurated at Yara Sluiskil, feeding the Northern Lights cross-border chain** — Yara officially opened a facility at its Sluiskil ammonia plant in the Netherlands that captures and liquefies up to 800,000 t/yr of CO₂, establishing what the partners call the first complete cross-border CCS value chain: the liquefied CO₂ is shipped by Northern Lights vessels to Øygarden, Norway, and stored ~2,600 m below the seabed (~12 Mt expected over 15 years). The inauguration drew Norwegian PM Jonas Gahr Støre, Dutch PM Rob Jetten, EU climate commissioner Wopke Hoekstra and Yara CEO Svein Tore Holsether. (Offshore Energy, 7 Sep 2026) [link](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/netherlands-becomes-home-to-europes-largest-carbon-capture-facility/)

## Technology

- **Airhive and Australian Carbon Vault win federal funding to build Australia's first DAC-and-storage hub** — UK-based direct-air-capture developer Airhive and Australian Carbon Vault (ACV) secured about A$10m (US$7.2m) of Australian Government funding — part of a broader ~A$32.6m (US$23.4m) carbon-removal awards round — to develop the country's first integrated direct-air-capture-and-storage (DACCS) hub, pairing Airhive's fluidised-bed capture (already deployed in Canada and the UK) with onshore geological storage in South Australia's Arckaringa Basin. (Carbon Herald, 7 Sep 2026) [link](https://carbonherald.com/airhive-and-acv-to-build-australias-first-daccs-hub-with-over-7m-govt-funding/)
