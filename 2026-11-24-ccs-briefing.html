EnEarth to take Storegga's 30% stake and Lead Developer role at Acorn CCS

Carbon Herald; Upstream; PFI · 23 Nov 2026 · [Europe/UK]

EnEarth, the carbon-storage business spun out of Energean, has signed a sale-and-purchase agreement to acquire Storegga's 30% working interest and its Lead Developer role in the Acorn CO₂ transport-and-storage project at St Fergus in north-east Scotland, with Storegga's dedicated Acorn team transferring across. Storegga flagged the exit after a December 2025 strategic review; EnEarth says it will fold Track-2-status Acorn into a pan-European CO₂ storage platform.
