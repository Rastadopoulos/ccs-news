Alfa Laval selected by Saipem to supply heat-transfer technology for Stockholm Exergi's BECCS plant

Alfa Laval / Hydrocarbon Processing · 15 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Alfa Laval won an order from EPC contractor Saipem to supply its Ziepack gas–gas interchanger and process plate heat exchangers for Stockholm Exergi's bioenergy carbon-capture-and-storage facility at the Värtaverket biomass CHP plant in Stockholm. The plant is designed to capture, liquefy and permanently store up to 800,000 tonnes of CO₂ a year from 2028, with the CO₂ shipped for storage via Norway's Northern Lights — one of Europe's largest BECCS projects.
