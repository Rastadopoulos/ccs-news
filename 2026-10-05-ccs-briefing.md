- **£200m carbon capture at Enfinium's Parc Adfer energy-from-waste plant recommended for approval** — Officers have recommended approval of a £200m carbon-capture extension to Enfinium's Parc Adfer energy-from-waste plant at Connah's Quay, Flintshire, ahead of a Flintshire County Council committee vote on 7 October; it would capture ~241,000 t/yr of CO₂ (95% capture) from 232,000 t of residual waste and feed the HyNet pipeline for storage at Point of Ayr beneath the Irish Sea. ## Markets & strategy

- **Northern Lights signs Öresundskraft as its second Swedish customer** — The Northern Lights JV (Equinor, Shell, TotalEnergies) agreed to transport and permanently store up to 200,000 t/yr of CO₂ from Öresundskraft's Filbornaverket waste-to-energy plant in Helsingborg; the CO₂ will be trucked to Halmstad and shipped to Øygarden, Norway, for sub-seabed storage from Q4 2029 — a second Swedish contract that takes Northern Lights to seven customers across four countries. (Carbon Capture Journal, 2 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.carboncapturejournal.com/news/resundskraft-reaches-fid-signs-with-northern-lights-for-co2-transport-and-storage/7433.aspx?Category=all)
- **Cowboy Clean Fuels makes its first durable carbon-removal sale in a Salesforce-backed round** — Wyoming's Cowboy Clean Fuels sold ~500 t of durable carbon-removal credits via Milkywire's Salesforce-backed purchasing round (3,661 t across eight suppliers); its BiCRS+RNG process injects agricultural residues into unmineable coal seams, where microbes convert the biomass into CO₂ that binds to the coal (claimed >100-year permanence) while also yielding renewable gas. (Carbon Credits, 3 Oct 2026) [link](https://carboncredits.com/cowboy-clean-fuels-durable-carbon-removal-salesforce/)
