## Projects

- **Air Liquide completes 'golden weld' connecting its Rotterdam capture plant to the Porthos CO2 pipeline** — Air Liquide made the "golden weld" physically tying its Cryocap cryogenic capture unit at Rozenburg into the shared Porthos CO2 transport-and-storage network in the Port of Rotterdam. Once operational, captured CO2 will run through the collective gathering pipeline to the Maasvlakte compressor station and offshore to the depleted P18-A gas field beneath the North Sea; Air Liquide is one of Porthos's four foundation customers (with Shell, Air Products and ExxonMobil), and the hub is targeted to start up in H2 2027 at the earliest. (Carbon Herald, 29 Sep 2026) [link](https://carbonherald.com/golden-weld-connects-air-liquide-to-porthos-co2-pipeline-in-rotterdam/)

## Markets & strategy

- **Climeworks wins ~£0.78m UK government tender to supply direct-air-capture removal credits** — Swiss direct-air-capture developer Climeworks won a £783,749 (~US$1.04m) UK government tender to supply carbon-removal credits used to offset construction emissions from a new government building, part of a broader ~£2.5m government carbon-credit procurement. The award is a modest but notable instance of public-sector procurement of durable, engineered removals rather than cheaper avoidance offsets. (Quantum Commodity Intelligence, 29 Sep 2026) [paywall] [link](https://www.qcintel.com/carbon/article/climeworks-wins-784-000-uk-govt-carbon-credit-tender-73983.html)
