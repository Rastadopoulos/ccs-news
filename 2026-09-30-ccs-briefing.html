Air Liquide completes 'golden weld' connecting its Rotterdam capture plant to the Porthos CO2 pipeline

Carbon Herald · 29 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Air Liquide made the "golden weld" physically tying its Cryocap cryogenic capture unit at Rozenburg into the shared Porthos CO2 transport-and-storage network in the Port of Rotterdam. Once operational, captured CO2 will run through the collective gathering pipeline to the Maasvlakte compressor station and offshore to the depleted P18-A gas field beneath the North Sea. Air Liquide is one of Porthos's four foundation customers (with Shell, Air Products and ExxonMobil), and the hub is targeted to start up in H2 2027 at the earliest.

Climeworks wins ~£0.78m UK government tender to supply direct-air-capture removal credits

Quantum Commodity Intelligence · 29 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK] · [paywall]

Swiss direct-air-capture developer Climeworks won a £783,749 (~US$1.04m) UK government tender to supply carbon-removal credits used to offset construction emissions from a new government building, part of a broader ~£2.5m government carbon-credit procurement. The award is a modest but notable instance of public-sector procurement of durable, engineered removals rather than cheaper avoidance offsets.

Rio Tinto and Shougang commission an industrial-scale blast-furnace CO2-capture trial at Jingtang, China

Rio Tinto / Reuters · 28 Sep 2026 · [China]

Rio Tinto and Chinese steelmaker Shougang commissioned an industrial-scale facility to capture CO2 from blast-furnace gas at Shougang's Jingtang base, able to process up to 3,000 m³/h of blast-furnace gas and capture up to 10,000 tonnes of CO2 a year, using waste heat to cut capture cost; the partners are also exploring converting captured CO2 into syngas for reuse in steelmaking. A rare industrial-scale steel-sector CCU catch, but the underlying announcement was 28 Sep — one day outside today's window.
