Oregon State–led team unveils water-tolerant MOFs that capture CO₂ from humid flue gas

EurekAlert / Oregon State University (Nature) · 6 Oct 2026 · [North America]

An international team co-led by Oregon State chemist Kyriakos Stylianou (with EPFL, Heriot-Watt, the University of Ottawa and the University of Granada) reported in Nature a new class of metal–organic frameworks that keep their CO₂ selectivity in wet flue gas by “spatially decoupling” CO₂ and H₂O into separate, non-competing adsorption sites. Data-mining more than 325,000 candidate MOFs surfaced two water-stable structures that outperform activated carbon and zeolite 13X and stay effective from concentrated flue gas down to ultra-dilute, DAC-relevant streams — addressing the humidity problem that otherwise forces costly flue-gas drying.

Skytree secures €1m Dutch state development loan for a geothermal-powered DAC ‘Skypark’ in Iceland

gasworld / NL Times · 6 Oct 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Amsterdam-based direct-air-capture developer Skytree secured a €1 million development loan from Dutch state-backed investor Invest International to fund the engineering, permitting and site-preparation phase of a geothermal-powered DAC “Skypark” in Iceland, extending the Skypark model it launched in the Netherlands (a June 2026 JV with Lingezegen Energy). The captured CO₂ is earmarked for greenhouse horticulture, the food industry and synthetic-fuel feedstock, with DAC installation and commercial start targeted for 2028.
