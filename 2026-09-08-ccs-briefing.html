Europe's largest industrial carbon-capture facility inaugurated at Yara Sluiskil, feeding the Northern Lights cross-border chain

Offshore Energy · 7 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK — Netherlands / Norway]

Yara officially opened a carbon-capture facility at its Sluiskil ammonia plant in the Netherlands that captures and liquefies up to 800,000 t/yr of CO₂ — described by the partners as the first complete cross-border CCS value chain. The liquefied CO₂ is transported by Northern Lights vessels to Øygarden, Norway, and stored roughly 2,600 m below the seabed, with about 12 Mt expected to be stored over 15 years. The inauguration drew Norwegian PM Jonas Gahr Støre, Dutch PM Rob Jetten, EU climate commissioner Wopke Hoekstra and Yara CEO Svein Tore Holsether. For CO2CRC: a live, operating template for capturing hard-to-abate industrial (ammonia) CO₂ and monetising a capture-in-one-country, store-in-another chain — directly analogous to Australia's cross-border ambitions (Bayu-Undan, Australia–Japan/Korea export concepts).

Airhive and Australian Carbon Vault win federal funding to build Australia's first DAC-and-storage hub

Carbon Herald · 7 Sep 2026 · [Australia & APAC — Australia]

UK-based direct-air-capture developer Airhive and Australian Carbon Vault (ACV) secured about A$10m (US$7.2m) of Australian Government funding — part of a broader ~A$32.6m (US$23.4m) carbon-removal awards round — to develop the country's first integrated direct-air-capture-and-storage (DACCS) hub. The project pairs Airhive's fluidised-bed capture technology, already deployed in Canada and the UK, with onshore geological storage in South Australia's Arckaringa Basin. For CO2CRC: a home-jurisdiction, first-of-a-kind DACCS deployment coupling engineered removal to Australian onshore storage — squarely in CO2CRC's technical and storage-characterisation wheelhouse and a marker of federal appetite to fund durable CDR.

Switzerland launches Europe's first commercial cross-border BECCS chain, shipping biogenic CO₂ to Greensand in Denmark

Carbon Herald · 2 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK — Switzerland / Denmark]

Airfix, CO2 Energie AG and South Pole began transporting liquefied biogenic CO₂ (up to ~4,300 t/yr) from the Regionalwerke AG Baden biomethane plant in Niederwil to INEOS-operated Project Greensand for permanent North Sea storage, at ~92% net removal efficiency — an early template for small-volume, ISO-container CO₂ export chains.
