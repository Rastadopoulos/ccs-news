Woodside exits Bonaparte CCS as INPEX takes full control and moves the project into FEED

Upstream / Energy News Bulletin · 25 Oct 2026 · [Australia]

INPEX has agreed to acquire Woodside Energy's 21% stake in the INPEX-led Bonaparte CCS joint venture (~260 km offshore Darwin), lifting INPEX's interest to 74%, with the holding transferred to a newly formed INPEX Bonaparte CCS Pty Ltd and completion subject to Australian regulatory approval; the joint venture (also TotalEnergies CCS Australia 26%) has now commenced front-end engineering and design, targeting first CO2 injection in the early 2030s with the Ichthys LNG joint venture as anchor customer (the scheme could cut Ichthys LNG's CO2 emissions by ~40%). Follows the 16 October declaration of the West Peron storage formation.

PETRONAS, TotalEnergies and Mitsui sign JV agreement to develop Southern CCS Hub storage area off Peninsular Malaysia

Offshore Energy / Xinhua / gasworld · 23 Oct 2026 · [Malaysia / APAC]

PETRONAS CCS Ventures, TotalEnergies EP CCS Malaysia and Mitsui & Co. signed a joint-venture agreement to form a dedicated company developing a CO2 storage area offshore Peninsular Malaysia, part of Malaysia's Southern CCS Hub; the first phase targets a depleted offshore gas field with up to 5 Mtpa of injection capacity and first injection as early as 2031 (subject to industry readiness), building on the trio's 2023 CCS Development Agreement and 2025 Key Principles Agreement, with PCCSV already holding an offshore assessment permit from Malaysia's CCUS agency. The hub is designed to receive CO2 both domestically and regionally — including potential cross-border CO2 shipments — making it a notable transboundary-CCS and multinational-strategy marker for the region.

Skytree, Galetech and Mwahera to add direct air capture to Kenya's Mwangea e-Fuels Project

Carbon Herald · 21 Oct 2026 · [Kenya / Africa] · [DAC]

The three partners signed an agreement to integrate Skytree's DAC technology into the Mwangea e-Fuels Project in Kenya, combining captured atmospheric CO2 with green hydrogen to make carbon-neutral e-fuels for aviation, maritime and long-haul transport; the scheme begins with an initial deployment phase and, once scaled, could capture up to tens of kilotonnes of CO2 a year, with Skytree claiming its mineral-based sorbent could bring costs below US$100/t. A rare African DAC catch, though the signing dates 21 October — just outside today's window.
