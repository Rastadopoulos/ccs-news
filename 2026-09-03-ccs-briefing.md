- **UK's Northern Endurance Partnership begins offshore pipelaying as Saipem's *Castorone* arrives on site** — Construction of the UK's first offshore CO₂ transport-and-storage network has reached the seabed: Saipem's 325 m pipelay vessel *Castorone* has started laying the ~145 km offshore pipeline that will connect Teesside industrial emitters (the East Coast Cluster) to the Endurance saline aquifer, ~1,000 m beneath the Southern North Sea, with the *Castoro 10* handling nearshore shallow-water sections. The system is designed to transport and store around 4 Mtpa of CO₂ from 2027–28, with storage expected to start in 2028 and up to 100 Mt stored over the initial 25-year phase. NEP is a joint venture of bp, Equinor and TotalEnergies. The UK's first offshore CCS T&S network moving into offshore installation is a live delivery benchmark for Australia's own offshore-storage ambitions (Bonaparte, Browse, CarbonNet).
