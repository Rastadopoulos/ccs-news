JFE Steel selected for the Nippon Foundation–DeepStar CO₂ transport pipeline R&D project

gasworld · 1 Sep 2026 · [Australia & APAC — Japan]

Japan's JFE Steel has been selected to join the Nippon Foundation–DeepStar joint research programme on CO₂ transport pipelines. The company will test its electric-resistance-welded "Mighty Seam" line pipe — with material testing at its Steel Research Laboratory in Chiba — to establish how impurities in captured CO₂ affect the strength and fracture resistance of pipeline steel, feeding evaluation methods and material requirements for CO₂ pipelines that underpin CCS/CCUS deployment. DeepStar is an offshore oil-and-gas research consortium whose members include ExxonMobil, Chevron and TotalEnergies. CO₂-stream impurity tolerance and pipeline metallurgy are a shared technical bottleneck for Australia's own offshore CO₂-transport ambitions.

Texas appellate court sends Occidental carbon-capture dispute to arbitration

Law360 · 1 Sep 2026 · [North America / Australia] · [paywall]

A Texas appellate court has revived Occidental Petroleum's bid to arbitrate a multi-million-dollar dispute arising from a project to deploy greenhouse-gas-reduction (carbon capture) technology in Australia, ruling that a tribunal in British Columbia is the appropriate venue and sending the claims to arbitration. Beyond the venue question, the ruling is a reminder of the contractual and commercial-dispute risk attaching to cross-border deployment of CCS technology — here with a direct Australian nexus.
