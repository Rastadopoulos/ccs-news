# CCS Intelligence Dashboard — Scope

Status: reliability v2 implemented and populated through the local collection stop after 28 July 2026.

## Purpose

The product supports two decisions: whether CO2CRC/CO2Tech strategy aligns with real CCS deployment, and whether board/external-stakeholder reporting is defensible. It separates what is operating or physically advancing from what was merely announced or reported in the news.

## Unit of analysis

The primary unit is a stable canonical entity: project chain, capture facility, transport network, storage site, hub/cluster, funding programme or policy instrument. Events are dated evidence attached to those entities. The archive remains complete, but articles do not create additive project, capacity or funding totals.

## Dashboard hierarchy

1. Data freshness and technical coverage.
2. Three-to-five decision conclusions since the previous build.
3. Deployment reality: official operating/construction baselines, unique stage changes and actual storage delivery.
4. Australia benchmark using authoritative stock measures; recent news is an overlay.
5. Funding and commercial support with strict category separation.
6. World map, defaulting to operating maturity; country discovery credit is non-additive.
7. Legislated mandates, government targets, project milestones and commercial deadlines.
8. Technology, MMV/MRV and CO2Tech signals.
9. Social licence using media/opinion/community records only.
10. Filterable evidence and complete event archive.
11. Collapsible glossary and methodology.

## Integrity rules

- Preserve original source values, units, dates, scope, derivations and confidence.
- Never silently blend IEA and GCCSI. Edition and methodology travel with every baseline.
- Never add incompatible capacity bases or nameplate to actual delivery.
- Never infer missing drawdown, actual performance or CCS-specific mixed-project capex as zero.
- Use physical project geography for additive regional totals; headquarters and multi-country discovery tags are separate.
- Treat EU-wide programmes as bloc-level, not as a country or invented member-state allocation.
- Separate event direction from editorial/media tone.
- Label article counts `monitoring/newsflow volume`, not deployment momentum.
- Treat collection failure as impaired coverage, not a quiet day.
- Treat recall as adjudicated diagnostic evidence, not a claim to know all CCS news.
- Neither IEA nor GCCSI is audited financial data.

## Current authoritative baseline position

- IEA CCUS Projects Database 2026: authenticated official workbook, 1,110 named projects; exact matches and rule-assisted review candidates are reported separately.
- GCCSI GSR 2025: current global totals and 47-row construction appendix; GSR 2024 retained only for the visibly older all-stage country map.
- London Register 2025: 46 projects, annual series through 2024, 384.6 Mt cumulative all-storage.

## Known gaps

- The IEA workbook contains more entities and components than the 30-entity local canonical seed. Twenty-nine curated names/components match exactly; 1,081 unresolved or candidate rows remain reviewable rather than being guessed into the local register.
- The canonical seed covers the material entities evidenced in the local corpus, not every global CCS project. Capacity-bearing unmatched events are queued for review.
- Actual annual project performance is sparse. Moomba has a known annualised value; missing Gorgon actual is not treated as zero.
- GCCSI 2025 lacks a full all-stage country table equivalent to 2024, so the map layer remains mixed-vintage but unmistakably labelled.
- Programme drawdown and actual spend are generally unpublished. The awards total is a lower bound.
- Collection remains impaired after the 31 July facts file because scheduled fact files for 1–3 August are missing. The 3 August build does not infer a quiet period from alert-only coverage.
- Industrial-emissions-relative benchmarking is omitted until a defensible like-for-like denominator is curated.
