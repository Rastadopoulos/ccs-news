## Projects

- **Entropy commissions Glacier co-generation carbon capture project in Alberta** — Entropy Inc. (Advantage Energy) commissioned its Glacier co-generation CCS project in Saddle Hills County, Alberta, adding a 15 MW co-gen turbine with integrated capture and targeting ~192,000 tpa of CO2 captured and permanently sequestered at capture rates above 90%; the company frames it as first-of-kind low-carbon natural gas plus low-carbon power, building on continuous commercial capture at Glacier since 2022. Backed by Advantage Energy, Brookfield and the Canada Growth Fund. (PR Newswire / BOE Report, 30 Jul 2026) [link](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/entropy-inc-commissions-the-glacier-co-generation-carbon-capture-and-storage-project-delivering-first-of-kind-low-carbon-natural-gas-and-low-carbon-power-302839526.html)
- **Heidelberg hails piling progress at the UK's first cement-works carbon-capture plant, Wales** — Heidelberg Materials reported more than 600 piles installed at its Padeswood cement CCS facility in Flintshire, north Wales, alongside ~60,000 t of aggregate laid and 25,000 t of topsoil removed, with the on-site workforce (~80) expected to double by year-end; the plant is designed to capture ~95% of the works' CO2 (~800,000 tpa), routing it via the HyNet pipeline to storage beneath Liverpool Bay, with operation targeted for 2029. EPC is delivered by Worley and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries following a Sep-2025 FID with the UK Government. (Ground Engineering, 31 Jul 2026) [link](https://www.geplus.co.uk/news/project-news/heidelberg-hails-piling-progress-on-carbon-capture-project-in-wales-31-07-2026/)
