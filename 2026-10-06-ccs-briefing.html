ExxonMobil adds Williams as its seventh CO₂ storage customer for the Louisiana Energy Gateway

ExxonMobil / Oil & Gas Journal · 5 Oct 2026 · [North America]

Williams has selected ExxonMobil to transport and permanently store up to 1 Mtpa of CO₂ from its proposed Louisiana Energy Gateway gas gathering and processing system (Haynesville gas routed to US Gulf Coast LNG). It is ExxonMobil's seventh commercial CCS customer and its fifth in Louisiana, lifting its contracted CO₂ transport-and-storage portfolio to about 9 Mtpa; the project remains subject to a final investment decision, permitting and other approvals.
