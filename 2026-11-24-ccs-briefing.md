## Projects
- **EnEarth to take Storegga's 30% stake and Lead Developer role at Acorn CCS** — EnEarth, the carbon-storage business spun out of Energean, has signed a sale-and-purchase agreement to acquire Storegga's 30% working interest and its Lead Developer role in the Acorn CO₂ transport-and-storage project at St Fergus in north-east Scotland, with Storegga's dedicated Acorn team transferring across. Storegga flagged the exit after a December 2025 strategic review; EnEarth says it will fold Track-2-status Acorn into a pan-European CO₂ storage platform. (Carbon Herald; Upstream; PFI, 23 Nov 2026) [link](https://carbonherald.com/storegga-exits-acorn-ccs-project-as-enearth-acquires-30-stake/)

## Policy
- **Study: a 'Carbon Takeback' obligation on North Sea producers could fund the first wave of UK CCS** — Trade coverage of new research argues a Carbon Takeback Obligation — requiring oil and gas producers to store (or pay to store) a rising share of the CO₂ embedded in their products — could bankroll early UK storage build-out without open-ended taxpayer subsidy, reviving a mechanism long debated alongside the UK ETS. (BusinessGreen, 23 Nov 2026) [link](https://www.businessgreen.com/news/4539324/study-carbon-takeback-north-sea-oil-gas-producers-fund-wave-uk-ccs-projects)
