## Projects

- **Eni completes the "world's first" purpose-built offshore CCS platform at Liverpool Bay (HyNet)** — Eni CCUS Holding (Eni with Global Infrastructure Partners) announced it has finished installing the new Douglas CCS platform in Liverpool Bay, the offshore hub for the UK's HyNet cluster. The platform will receive CO₂ captured from industrial sites across North West England and North Wales via the Point of Ayr gas terminal and inject it into the depleted Hamilton, Hamilton North and Lennox reservoirs through repurposed offshore pipelines. Phase 1 is sized for up to ~4.5 Mtpa of CO₂ (with potential expansion toward ~10 Mtpa in the 2030s) and operations are targeted for 2028; Eni describes it as the world's first offshore platform purpose-built for CO₂ transport and storage. A useful reference point for Australia's own offshore depleted-reservoir storage ambitions (CarbonNet, Bonaparte, Bayu-Undan). (Eni, 8 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.eni.com/en-IT/media/press-release/2026/10/eni-ccus-holding-liverpool-bay-ccs.html)
