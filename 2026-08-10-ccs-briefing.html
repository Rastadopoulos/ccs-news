Kansai Electric and Kawasaki Heavy to trial Japan's first off-site transport of captured CO2

Nikkei Asia · 8 Aug 2026 · [APAC]

The two firms plan to move CO2 captured at Kansai's Maizuru coal-fired power plant to a remote site as early as this fiscal year, working to prove out an off-site capture–transport–storage chain. It builds on their 40 t-CO2/day Kawasaki CO2 Capture (KCC) solid-sorbent demonstration running at Maizuru since 2020.

EU's first offshore CCS project, Denmark's Greensand, nears launch

Upstream · 7 Aug 2026 · [Europe & UK] · [paywall]

Upstream reports that the launch of INEOS Energy's Project Greensand — CO2 injection into depleted reservoirs in the Danish North Sea, the EU's first offshore CO2 store — is "imminent", ramping from an initial ~0.4 Mtpa toward multi-million-tonne capacity as the shipped-CO2 value chain via Port Esbjerg comes online.
