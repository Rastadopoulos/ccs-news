Shearwater wins offshore-Australia CO₂-storage seismic survey (Bonaparte Basin)

Upstream · 29 Jul 2026 · [Australia / APAC]

Shearwater GeoServices has been awarded a 3D seismic survey (vessel SW Bly, ~50-day campaign starting Q3 2026) to appraise carbon-storage potential in the Bonaparte Basin for INPEX's proposed CCS project. The data will high-grade future injection-well locations and provide a baseline for later 4D monitoring. It is Shearwater's second Bonaparte CCS survey after a 2024 campaign, with first injection targeted around 2030.

Northern Lights completes its phase-1 LCO₂ carrier fleet as 'Northern Purpose' reaches Norway

Offshore Engineer / MarineLink · 29 Jul 2026 · [Europe / Norway]

The fourth dedicated 7,500 m³ liquefied-CO₂ carrier, Northern Purpose, arrived in Norway on 29 July following delivery from Dalian, completing the four-vessel fleet (Pioneer, Pathfinder, Phoenix, Purpose) that will ship captured CO₂ from European emitters to the Øygarden receiving terminal for pipeline transport to offshore storage. Fleet completion positions the Equinor/Shell/TotalEnergies JV to scale phase-1 customer operations (~1.5 Mtpa).
