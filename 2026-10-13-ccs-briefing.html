UK start-up Parallel Carbon pairs direct air capture with green-hydrogen electrolysis to chase sub-US$100/t CO₂

Chemical & Engineering News (C&EN / ACS) · 12 Oct 2026 · [Europe & UK]

In a C&EN "start-ups to watch" profile, Manchester-based Parallel Carbon describes a membraneless electrolyser that splits a neutral-salt electrolyte into acid and alkali (without chlorine) to co-produce green hydrogen while a mineral sorbent captures CO₂ from air and is regenerated by the acid stream. The company targets capture below US$100/tonne and hydrogen at ~US$1/kg, runs a small pilot near Manchester, and has a pre-purchase agreement with Zurich Insurance for 1,200 tonnes of durable removal. Figures are company targets, not independently verified. A novel low-cost DAC + hydrogen co-production route worth tracking against CO2CRC's capture-materials and DAC research remit.
