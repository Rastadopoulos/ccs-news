## Markets & strategy

- **Höegh Evi and Aker BP win €223m EU grant for cross-border CO2 shipping-and-storage chain** — The partners secured a €223m EU Innovation Fund grant for their joint scheme to collect captured CO2 from north-west European emitters — via floating CO2 infrastructure and next-generation liquefied-CO2 carriers — and ship it to permanent storage in Aker BP-operated reservoirs on the Norwegian Continental Shelf (initial sizing up to ~10 Mtpa). A notable public-funding vote of confidence in liquefied-CO2 shipping as a cross-border storage enabler. (TradeWinds, 21 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.tradewindsnews.com/gas/hoegh-evi-and-aker-bp-bag-223m-from-eu-fund-for-carbon-capture-project/2-1-2061470)
