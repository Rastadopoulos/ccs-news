Vaulted Deep secures US$35m offtake-backed debt facility to scale biomass carbon removal

ESG Dive / Vaulted Deep · 22 Sep 2026 · [North America]

US bio-waste carbon-removal developer Vaulted Deep closed a US$35m debt facility from Italy's Mediobanca, arranged by UK trading firm CFP Energy and secured against long-term removal and waste-servicing contracts with Frontier Climate buyers (Google, Stripe and others). The company calls it the largest publicly disclosed US commercial debt deal in durable CDR backed by long-term purchase contracts, and says it delivered 20,000 tonnes of removals to Frontier buyers in the first half of 2026 — more than its full-year 2025 total. The signal for CCS/CDR watchers is financial structure rather than tonnage: durable-removal offtakes are now bankable enough to raise non-dilutive debt against — a template Australian CDR and storage developers will watch as they pursue project finance.
