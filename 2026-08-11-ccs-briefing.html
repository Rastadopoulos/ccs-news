KAIST and MIT turn seawater CO₂ into "stone" for permanent storage

phys.org / EurekAlert · 4 Aug 2026 · [APAC]

Researchers led by KAIST's Dong-Yeun Koh with MIT's T. Alan Hatton group unveiled an electrochemical dissolved-ocean-carbon (e-DOC) process that converts CO₂ dissolved in seawater into solid calcium carbonate for durable storage, letting the ocean draw down more atmospheric CO₂; published in Advanced Energy Materials and funded in part by Hyundai Motor and Kia.
