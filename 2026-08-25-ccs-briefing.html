JOGMEC signs six-company consortium to design ship-based Mizushima CCS cluster — Japan's largest planned CO₂ shipping hub

Sumitomo Corp / Carbon Pulse · 24 Aug 2026 · [Japan / APAC]

Japan's state minerals-and-energy agency JOGMEC has signed a design-work agreement with a consortium of JFE Steel, Sumitomo Corporation, Asahi Kasei, ENEOS, Mitsubishi Gas Chemical and Mitsubishi Chemical to develop an advanced ship-based CCS project at the Mizushima Industrial Complex in Kurashiki, Okayama. The consortium will study facilities for CO₂ separation, capture, liquefaction, temporary storage and shipping; the cluster is designed to handle about 3.43 Mtpa of liquefied CO₂, which JOGMEC says would make it the largest planned CO₂ shipping hub among the CCS clusters currently in development. The award follows JOGMEC's FY2026 public solicitation to cut CCS costs by aggregating adjacent hard-to-abate emitters into shared liquefaction-and-shipping clusters — the demand side of the maritime-transport CCS model that would send captured CO₂ to offshore storage, including potentially in Australia.
