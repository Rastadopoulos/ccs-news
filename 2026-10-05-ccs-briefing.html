£200m carbon capture at Enfinium's Parc Adfer energy-from-waste plant recommended for approval

The Leader (Wrexham) · 3 Oct 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Officers have recommended approval of a £200m carbon-capture extension to Enfinium's Parc Adfer energy-from-waste plant at Connah's Quay, Flintshire, ahead of a Flintshire County Council committee vote on 7 October. The facility would capture about 241,000 tonnes of CO₂ a year (95% capture) from 232,000 tonnes of residual waste and feed the HyNet pipeline for storage at Point of Ayr beneath the Irish Sea.

Northern Lights signs Öresundskraft as its second Swedish customer

Carbon Capture Journal · 2 Oct 2026 · [Europe & UK]

The Northern Lights JV (Equinor, Shell, TotalEnergies) agreed to transport and permanently store up to 200,000 tonnes of CO₂ a year from Öresundskraft's Filbornaverket waste-to-energy plant in Helsingborg. The CO₂ will be trucked to Halmstad and shipped to Øygarden, Norway, for sub-seabed storage from Q4 2029 — a second Swedish contract that takes Northern Lights to seven customers across four countries.

Cowboy Clean Fuels makes its first durable carbon-removal sale in a Salesforce-backed round

Carbon Credits · 3 Oct 2026 · [North America]

Wyoming's Cowboy Clean Fuels sold about 500 tonnes of durable carbon-removal credits via Milkywire's Salesforce-backed purchasing round (3,661 tonnes across eight suppliers). Its BiCRS+RNG process injects agricultural residues into unmineable coal seams, where microbes convert the biomass into CO₂ that binds to the coal (claimed >100-year permanence) while also producing renewable gas.
