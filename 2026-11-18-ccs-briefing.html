CNPC commissions what it calls China's longest CO₂ pipeline for Jilin CCUS-EOR

Carbon Herald; gasworld · 17 Nov 2026 · [China]

PetroChina/CNPC has put into operation a long-distance dense-phase CO₂ pipeline feeding its Jilin oilfield CCUS-EOR complex in the Songliao Basin, north-east China. The first phase runs about 282 km with a design capacity of roughly 3.3 Mt CO₂/yr (the planned full route is near 400 km linking Changchun, Jilin, Songyuan and Siping), extending CNPC's commercial CCUS-EOR push — the company has cumulatively injected more than 13 Mt CO₂.

COP31 draft cover decision sets first-ever 2035 CCUS and carbon-removal capacity goals

Carbon Herald · 17 Nov 2026 · [Global]

The Türkiye COP31 Presidency's first draft cover decision proposes, for the first time in a COP text, quantified scale-up goals for carbon management — at least ~500 Mt CO₂/yr of CCUS capacity by 2035, alongside a longer-term ambition cited at 1.3–2.5 Gt/yr. The Presidency stressed the draft reflects divergent national positions, not consensus, and EU climate chief Wopke Hoekstra dismissed it as a “shopping list” at odds with the logic of implementation; adoption is targeted for the closing plenary on Friday.
