Höegh Evi and Aker BP win €223m EU grant for cross-border CO2 shipping-and-storage chain

TradeWinds · 21 Oct 2026 · [Europe/UK]

The partners secured a €223m EU Innovation Fund grant for their joint scheme to collect captured CO2 from north-west European emitters — via floating CO2 infrastructure and next-generation liquefied-CO2 carriers — and ship it to permanent storage in Aker BP-operated reservoirs on the Norwegian Continental Shelf (initial sizing up to ~10 Mtpa). A notable public-funding vote of confidence in liquefied-CO2 shipping as a cross-border storage enabler.
