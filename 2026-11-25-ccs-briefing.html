Ozona CCS and Marubeni reach FID on Texas's Pawnee carbon sequestration project

JPT/SPE; BIC Magazine · 24 Nov 2026 · [North America]

The 50/50 Ozona–Marubeni joint venture has taken a final investment decision on the Pawnee project in Bee County, South Texas, which will capture CO₂ from a StarTex Field Services gas-treating facility and store up to 400,000 t/yr in the Sligo Formation, with first injection targeted for Q1 2027. Ozona builds and operates; Marubeni — a Japanese trading house — leads carbon-credit marketing, and the partners say the project can scale as more CO₂ sources connect.
