CNPC passes 13 Mt of cumulative CO2 injected as it pushes CCUS commercialisation

Carbon Herald · 28 Sep 2026 · [China]

China National Petroleum Corp says it has now injected more than 13 million tonnes of CO2 to date — over 60% of the Chinese petroleum sector's total — with first-half 2026 injection of 1.37 Mt (up 14.2% year-on-year), mostly via CO2-EOR. It plans integrated industrial CCUS clusters in the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026–30), with the Junggar Basin area estimated to hold up to 2 Gt of storage.

US BLM offers Tallgrass a right-of-way grant for Southeast Wyoming CO2 storage

Carbon Herald · 28 Sep 2026 · [North America]

The US Bureau of Land Management has offered Tallgrass Energy a right-of-way grant to use 480 acres of federally managed underground pore space in Laramie County for its Southeast Wyoming CO2 Sequestration Project, advancing commercial use of federal pore space. Final Class VI injection-well permits from the Wyoming Department of Environmental Quality are still required before injection can begin.

Revcoo brings all-electric cryogenic capture to a Belgian zinc plant in its first metals-sector deal

gasworld · 28 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK]

French carbon-capture firm Revcoo will deploy its fully electric cryogenic "CarbonCloud" system at recycled-zinc producer Reazn's alloy plant in Kruisem, Belgium, targeting 5,000–8,000 tpa of 99.99%-purity CO2 without solvents or water. The partners begin with a joint technical and economic feasibility study and FEED, with full operation slated for late 2028 — Revcoo's first move beyond lime and cement into metals.
