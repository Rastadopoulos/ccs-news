Canada, Alberta and Oil Sands Alliance agree to advance the Pathways CCS project

Reuters · 1 October 2026 · [North America]

Prime Minister Mark Carney announced in Fort McMurray that the federal and Alberta governments and the five-member Oil Sands Alliance have reached an agreement to advance one of the world's largest CCS projects — roughly 6 Mtpa of captured CO2 by the mid-2030s, rising to 16 Mtpa by 2045 — with Ottawa extending investment tax credits (50% on capture equipment, 37.5% on transport and storage) to 2035. A final investment decision is still not expected until late 2027 or early 2028, and the deal is tied to federal backing for the Pacific Link oil-export pipeline.
