Germany signs first bilateral CO₂ export agreements with Denmark and the Netherlands

Carbon Herald · 6 Oct 2026 · [Europe & UK]

At the Carbon Management Forum's Strategic Dialogue in Berlin (signed 5–6 Oct), Germany concluded its first government-to-government arrangements letting German industry export CO₂ for permanent sub-seabed storage beneath the Danish and Dutch North Sea. Germany bans onshore storage, so the deals open the key export route for German emitters and are framed around enabling DACCS/BioCCS removals; they follow the London Protocol Article 6 model. A direct precedent worth watching for Australia–Timor-Leste (Bayu-Undan) cross-border CO₂ export. Also reported by Carbon Pulse [paywall] and Montel.
