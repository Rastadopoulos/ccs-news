Entropy commissions Glacier co-generation carbon capture project in Alberta

PR Newswire / BOE Report · 30 Jul 2026 · [North America]

Entropy Inc. (Advantage Energy) commissioned its Glacier co-generation CCS project in Saddle Hills County, Alberta, adding a 15 MW co-gen turbine with integrated capture and targeting ~192,000 tpa of CO2 captured and permanently sequestered at capture rates above 90%; the company frames it as first-of-kind low-carbon natural gas plus low-carbon power, building on continuous commercial capture at Glacier since 2022. Heidelberg hails piling progress at the UK's first cement-works carbon-capture plant, Wales

Ground Engineering · 31 Jul 2026 · [Europe/UK]

Heidelberg Materials reported more than 600 piles installed at its Padeswood cement CCS facility in Flintshire, north Wales, alongside ~60,000 t of aggregate laid and 25,000 t of topsoil removed, with the on-site workforce (~80) expected to double by year-end; the plant is designed to capture ~95% of the works' CO2 (~800,000 tpa), routing it via the HyNet pipeline to storage beneath Liverpool Bay, with operation targeted for 2029. EPC is delivered by Worley and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries following a Sep-2025 FID with the UK Government.

Energy Intelligence: uncertainty plagues the US DAC sector as players shift strategy

Energy Intelligence · 31 Jul 2026 · [North America] · [paywall]

An Energy Intelligence analysis charts the US direct-air-capture sector bifurcating sharply amid policy uncertainty — a freeze on the DOE's US$3.5bn DAC Hubs programme and a proposed 45Q cut from $180 to $36/t for DAC — with buyers and developers concentrating in states with stable incentives (California, New York, Washington), well-capitalised integrated players such as Occidental proving resilient, and Canada and Kenya emerging as alternative DAC destinations.
