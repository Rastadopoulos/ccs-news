- **INPEX begins first CO₂ injection at Kashiwazaki Hydrogen Park — Japan's first fully integrated blue-hydrogen/ammonia demonstration with CCS** — INPEX has commenced CO₂ injection into the depleted Higashi-Kashiwazaki gas field (Hirai District, Niigata Prefecture) as part of its blue hydrogen and ammonia production-and-usage demonstration, the first domestic project spanning the entire chain from low-carbon H₂/NH₃ manufacture to end use. - **Woodside's Browse CCS cleared for full assessment under the amended EPBC Act** — The federal Environment Minister has determined that Woodside's resubmitted Browse CCS referral will be assessed wholly under the amended Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 — a procedural step that puts the project back on a defined federal assessment pathway after Woodside withdrew its earlier referral pending the Act's reform. The CCS scheme would inject CO₂ from the Browse fields into the offshore Calliance Storage Formation (GHG Assessment Permit G-8-AP, granted 2022), which Woodside says would cut project emissions by ~53 Mt CO₂e versus its 2019 Scope 1 estimate. Central to whether the long-contested Browse gas development proceeds. ## Policy

- **Tangguh CCUS set to become the first CCS project registered under the Indonesia–Japan Joint Crediting Mechanism** — The Indonesia–Japan JCM Joint Committee has approved the Ofaweri decarbonisation initiative built on the BP-operated Tangguh CCUS project (West Papua) to advance as a JCM project, making Tangguh the first CCUS project registered under the bilateral crediting framework — the mechanism that would let captured-and-stored CO₂ generate cross-border carbon credits shared between the two countries.
