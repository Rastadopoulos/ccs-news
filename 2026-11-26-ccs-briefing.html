Australia and Singapore conclude 'first-of-its-kind' cross-border CCS agreement

Upstream; Petromindo · 25 Nov 2026 · [APAC]

The two governments have reached a first-of-its-kind bilateral cross-border CCS instrument under their Green Economy Agreement, intended to let captured CO₂ from Singapore be shipped to and stored in Australian offshore reservoirs — positioned as a potential pathfinder for cross-border CCS across the Asia-Pacific/ASEAN and resting on Australia's provisional application of the London Protocol Article 6 amendment.

Malaysia launches CCUS Roadmap 2050, projecting up to RM170 billion and six hubs

The Edge Malaysia; Malay Mail; The Borneo Post · 25 Nov 2026 · [APAC]

Malaysia's Economy Ministry unveiled its "Malaysia CCUS Roadmap 2050", projecting up to RM170 billion (~A$58bn) in cumulative CCUS investment, up to six CCUS hubs by 2050, roughly 27 initiatives, and the potential for up to 250,000 jobs — a phased national framework following the recently enacted CCUS Act.
