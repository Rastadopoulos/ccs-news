Cool Planet Technologies officially opens membrane CO2 capture demonstration plant at Holcim's Höver cement works

Carbon Herald · 7 Sep 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Cool Planet held the official opening of its end-of-pipe, multilayer-polymer-membrane capture unit (~10,000 t/yr) at Holcim Germany's Höver cement plant in Lower Saxony. The membrane technology, developed with Helmholtz-Zentrum Hereon, is designed to eventually capture ~90% of the plant's ~500,000 t/yr of CO2 and to retrofit existing cement plants without reworking the production process.
