MOL, Hitachi and JAL to trial Japan’s first Direct Ocean Capture project off Kumejima

Carbon Herald · 4 Aug 2026 · [APAC — Japan]

Mitsui O.S.K. Lines, Hitachi and Japan Airlines signed an MoU to run Japan’s first Direct Ocean Capture (DOC) demonstration on Kume Island, Okinawa: a land-based, container-type unit will draw in seawater, strip out dissolved CO₂ and discharge the treated water, gathering baseline operating and coastal-environmental data. The three partners — earlier investors in US DOC developer Captura — will also explore using the recovered CO₂ for synthetic aviation fuel (e-SAF). No capacity figure was disclosed.

Hungary’s parliament abolishes its CO₂ quota tax to ease EU ETS burden and unlock EU funds

Carbon Herald · 4 Aug 2026 · [Europe/UK — Hungary]

Lawmakers voted on 4 Aug to scrap the 2023 CO₂ quota tax, a national levy on revenue from selling EU ETS free allowances that had stripped much of the value from those allocations; companies that paid it become eligible for refunds. The repeal follows the Court of Justice ruling in C-519/24 (16 Apr 2026) that the tax breached EU law, and forms part of a tax package Hungary must pass by 31 Aug 2026 to access blocked EU funds. CCS relevance is indirect — it restores the full carbon-price signal that underpins the economics of CCS and other abatement investment in Hungary.
