JAPEX starts second exploratory well as it nears FID on flagship Tomakomai CCS project

Upstream / Reuters · 17 Aug 2026 · [APAC — Japan] · [paywall]

Japan Petroleum Exploration has begun drilling a second exploratory well — an extended-reach well from a rig in Tomakomai, Hokkaido — to confirm the existence of geological formations suitable for CO₂ storage. Data from this well and the first exploratory well will support a final investment decision on the Tomakomai CCS project, which JAPEX is targeting within FY2026, with CO₂ storage slated to begin around FY2030. The project would capture CO₂ from Idemitsu Kosan's Hokkaido refinery and Hokkaido Electric Power's station, and is now run through the dedicated Tomakomai CCS Co. (TCCS) entity established on 1 July 2026.

US Treasury and IRS extend and expand the Section 45Q safe harbour (Notice 2026-50) (14 Aug 2026)

Carbon Herald · 14 Aug 2026 · [North America — United States]

Treasury and the IRS issued Notice 2026-50, extending and broadening the safe harbour for the federal Section 45Q carbon-capture tax credit and adding an independent-engineer certification path. The move responds to an EPA proposal to scrap key Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program requirements — including the Subpart RR protocols used to document geologic storage — and steadies the economics of the world's largest CCS incentive.
