Cheshire East Council unanimously passed a motion formally opposing the proposed Peak Cluster CO2 pipeline — a Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project still at pre-application stage — demanding independent environmental, hydrological, economic and safety assessments of the route options before any development consent application is determined, and asking for its concerns to be put to the Planning Inspectorate.
