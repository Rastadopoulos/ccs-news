## Projects

- **Australia declares West Peron storage formation for INPEX-led Bonaparte CCS — a national first for an offshore saline aquifer** — The federal government formally declared the West Peron formation (permit G-7-AP, ~260 km off Darwin) an identified greenhouse gas storage formation, the first such declaration in Australia for an offshore saline aquifer; INPEX plans to inject up to ~443 Mt of CO2 (cited at roughly 8–10 Mtpa) over ~35 years, with first CO2 from Ichthys LNG and first injection targeted early 2030s. The joint venture is INPEX Browse E&P (53%), TotalEnergies CCS Australia (26%) and Woodside Energy (21%); INPEX has flagged ambitions to turn Bonaparte into a global carbon-trade hub taking CO2 from Japan and Taiwan. (Upstream, 16 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.upstreamonline.com/carbon-capture/australias-first-offshore-ccs-project-wins-reservoir-approval/2-1-2059760)
- **Worley Rosenberg wins Subsea7 fabrication contract for Northern Lights Phase II** — Worley's Rosenberg yard will fabricate subsea spools and structures for the Phase II expansion of Norway's Northern Lights (Longship) project, which lifts CO2 transport-and-storage capacity from 1.5 Mtpa to more than 5 Mtpa by 2028; Subsea7 is Equinor's technical service provider for the phase (a ~5 km CO2 pipeline plus satellite structures and tie-ins). Contract value was not disclosed. (Upstream, 16 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.upstreamonline.com/carbon-capture/contractor-picks-up-fabrication-deal-for-major-norway-ccs-project/2-1-2060218)
- **One Earth Energy scales back Illinois CO2 storage project to a single injection well** — The ethanol producer's subsidiary cut its eastern McLean County plan from three injection wells to one (plus four monitoring wells and a 5.4-mile, 8-inch pipeline near Saybrook) as the project revives after Illinois' two-year CCS permit moratorium expired; it still needs an EPA Class VI permit and an Illinois Commerce Commission certificate amid ongoing Mahomet Aquifer safety objections. (WGLT, 16 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.wglt.org/local-news/2026-10-16/one-earth-energy-scales-back-proposed-carbon-capture-proposal-in-eastern-mclean-county)

## Markets & strategy

- **Frontier buyers sign ~US$11.7M in-situ mineralisation removal deal with Oman's 44.01** — Frontier's advance-market buyers (Stripe, Google, Shopify, Autodesk, H&M Group, Workday and others via Watershed) committed about US$11.7M for ~20,932 tonnes of CO2 removal delivered 2028–2031 using 44.01's peridotite mineralisation; it is Frontier's first in-situ mineralisation purchase, capturing CO2 from biogas upgrading and injecting carbonated water into fractured peridotite in Oman/UAE. (Carbon Credits, 16 Oct 2026) [link](https://carboncredits.com/frontier-signs-11-7m-carbon-removal-deal-with-44-01s-rock-mineralization-technology/)
- **Wood Mackenzie: global CO2 transport sector to reach ~US$211bn by 2060, with APAC ~60% of spend** — A new Wood Mackenzie report projects ~US$211bn of global CO2 transport investment by 2060, enabling about 3 Gtpa of CO2 to be moved with ~200 Mtpa crossing borders; Asia Pacific accounts for roughly 60% of investment given long distances and large volumes, pipelines dominate, and the mismatch between CO2 volumes and domestic storage is driving cross-border CO2 trade and liquefied-CO2 shipping. (Wood Mackenzie, 16 Oct 2026) [link](https://www.woodmac.com/press-releases/global-co2-transport-industry-set-to-reach-usd-211-billion-by-2060/)
