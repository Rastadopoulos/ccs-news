Projects

Northern Endurance Partnership launches offshore construction at the Port of Hartlepool

Energy Voice · 24 July 2026 · [Europe & UK]

The Northern Endurance Partnership — the UK's first CO₂ transport-and-storage network (BP, Equinor, TotalEnergies), delivered with Saipem — began its offshore construction campaign at PD Ports' Port of Hartlepool, staging ~12,000 concrete-coated linepipe joints (~170,000 t) for a 149 km, 28-inch offshore pipeline; more than 60% of the joints were made in Hartlepool. Once operational the network will transport and store up to 23 Mtpa of CO₂ — around 20% of the UK's annual emissions — from Teesside and Humber industry.

BP's Tangguh Ubadari CCUS moves into offshore installation as first jacket reaches Papua

Offshore Engineer · 24 July 2026 · [Australia & APAC]

The first of four offshore platform jackets (~994 t) for BP's Tangguh Ubadari (UCC) project completed its ~5,800 km journey from the Karimun yard to Fakfak, Papua Barat, marking the start of the offshore installation phase of Indonesia's first at-scale CCUS development. The ~US$7 billion scheme pairs Ubadari gas development with enhanced gas recovery and CO₂ storage, targeting ~15 Mt CO₂ sequestration in its initial phase, with 75 km of pipeline to the Tangguh LNG facilities to follow.

Uniper names Worley preferred EPC contractor for the Connah's Quay low-carbon (CCGT+CCS) power project

Carbon Herald · 23 July 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Uniper selected Worley as preferred EPC contractor for its proposed Connah's Quay Low Carbon Power project in Deeside, Wales — a combined-cycle gas turbine station (~1.1 GW, up to 1.38 GW; ~550 MW phase 1 potentially operational by 2030) fitted with carbon capture designed to trap at least 95% of CO₂ and connected to the HyNet CO₂ transport-and-storage network. The selection follows a FEED competition; a Development Consent Order decision is pending.

Evero moves to retrofit its Ince Bio Power plant with carbon capture, joining HyNet (UK BECCS)

gasworld · 24 July 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Evero Energy is progressing plans to retrofit its Ince Bio Power plant at Protos, Cheshire, with CO₂ capture — a BECCS project removing ~217,000 t CO₂/yr while processing ~170,000 t of waste wood — using Mitsubishi Heavy Industries capture technology and connecting to HyNet via the planned ~980 kt/yr Protos Spur pipeline. The planning application is before Cheshire West and Chester Council, with operation targeted around the end of the decade.

Petrogas picks Worley for pre-FEED of the Abeona CO₂ storage project, Dutch North Sea

Offshore Energy · 23 July 2026 · [Europe & UK]

Petrogas E&P Netherlands appointed Worley to deliver the pre-FEED study for the Abeona CCS project (named after the Roman goddess of outward journeys) — a CO₂ transport-and-storage scheme for hard-to-abate emitters in the Netherlands' North Sea Canal Area that would reuse an existing pipeline from the Port of Amsterdam to inject CO₂ into depleted offshore reservoirs. Worley will refine the development concept and cost estimates ahead of FEED.
