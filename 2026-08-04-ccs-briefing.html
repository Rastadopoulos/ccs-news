Thailand and Canada pilot mobile carbon capture in the cement industry

The Manila Times / TCMA · 3 Aug 2026 · [APAC]

The Thai Cement Manufacturers Association (TCMA), with Canadian partners including the Government of Saskatchewan and the University of Regina (and support from ECCC, the GCCA and UNIDO), will install a Mobile Carbon Capture Unit (MCCU) at a TCMA member cement plant in the Saraburi "sandbox". The pilot is aimed at building technological readiness for capturing emissions from cement — a hard-to-abate sector — in support of Thailand's 2050 Net Zero Cement and Concrete Roadmap.
