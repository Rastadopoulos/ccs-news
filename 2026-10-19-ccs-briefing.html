Australia declares West Peron storage formation for INPEX-led Bonaparte CCS — a national first for an offshore saline aquifer

Upstream · 16 October 2026 · [Australia / APAC]

The federal government formally declared the West Peron formation (permit G-7-AP, ~260 km off Darwin) an identified greenhouse gas storage formation — the first such declaration in Australia for an offshore saline aquifer. INPEX plans to inject up to ~443 Mt of CO2 (cited at roughly 8–10 Mtpa) over about 35 years, with first CO2 from Ichthys LNG and first injection targeted for the early 2030s. The joint venture is INPEX Browse E&P (53%), TotalEnergies CCS Australia (26%) and Woodside Energy (21%); INPEX has flagged ambitions to turn Bonaparte into a global carbon-trade hub taking CO2 from Japan and Taiwan.

Worley Rosenberg wins Subsea7 fabrication contract for Northern Lights Phase II

Upstream · 16 October 2026 · [Europe / Norway]

Worley's Rosenberg yard will fabricate subsea spools and structures for the Phase II expansion of Norway's Northern Lights (Longship) project, which lifts CO2 transport-and-storage capacity from 1.5 Mtpa to more than 5 Mtpa by 2028. Subsea7 is Equinor's technical service provider for the phase, with scope covering a ~5 km CO2 pipeline plus satellite structures and tie-ins. The contract value was not disclosed.

One Earth Energy scales back Illinois CO2 storage project to a single injection well

WGLT · 16 October 2026 · [North America / USA]

The ethanol producer's subsidiary cut its eastern McLean County plan from three injection wells to one — plus four monitoring wells and a 5.4-mile, 8-inch pipeline near Saybrook — as the project revives after Illinois' two-year CCS permit moratorium expired. It still needs an EPA Class VI permit and an Illinois Commerce Commission certificate, amid ongoing objections tied to protecting the Mahomet Aquifer.

Frontier buyers sign ~US$11.7M in-situ mineralisation removal deal with Oman's 44.01

Carbon Credits · 16 October 2026 · [Middle East / Global]

Frontier's advance-market buyers (Stripe, Google, Shopify, Autodesk, H&M Group, Workday and others via Watershed) committed about US$11.7M for roughly 20,932 tonnes of CO2 removal delivered 2028–2031 using 44.01's peridotite mineralisation. It is Frontier's first in-situ mineralisation purchase, capturing CO2 from biogas upgrading and injecting carbonated water into fractured peridotite in Oman/UAE.

Wood Mackenzie: global CO2 transport sector to reach ~US$211bn by 2060, with APAC ~60% of spend

Wood Mackenzie · 16 October 2026 · [Global / APAC]

A new Wood Mackenzie report projects about US$211bn of global CO2 transport investment by 2060, enabling roughly 3 Gtpa of CO2 to be moved with ~200 Mtpa crossing borders. Asia Pacific accounts for around 60% of investment given long distances and large volumes; pipelines dominate, and the mismatch between CO2 volumes and domestic storage is driving cross-border CO2 trade and liquefied-CO2 shipping.
